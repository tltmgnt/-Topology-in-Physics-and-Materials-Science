# Project working agreement

Complete authorized implementation through tests and installed-package verification.
Preserve explicit energy units, fixed SSH/unit-cell conventions and unresolved gap
states. Keep bulk invariants, finite-size localization diagnostics and experimental
validation distinct. Do not infer protected states or real-material performance from
threshold candidates. Run known-limit spectral and numerical-failure tests after
relevant changes. Keep software/backend replay limits explicit. Only synthetic model
inputs may be uploaded by CI. Preserve the original license and never grant equipment
or experimental authorization through a passing report.
