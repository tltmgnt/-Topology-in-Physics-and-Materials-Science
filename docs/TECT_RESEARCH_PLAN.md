## Phase I exits

M1 formal semantics: specified for this finite prototype; broader mathematical framework remains a research hypothesis. M2 graph and M3 controller: implemented and tested. M4 adversarial engine: controller probes operational; domain adversarial coverage remains incomplete. M5 benchmark: observation contracts and descriptive metrics implemented; baseline experiments not collected. M6 pilot: synthetic adapter executes; claim intentionally remains unassured. M7 feasibility report: engineering verification is delivered in tests/CI, while comparative scientific benefit is unmeasured.

## Proposed study

Use matched tasks across human researchers with conventional tools, a single model, an unstructured multi-agent workflow and TECT-controlled workflow. Define claims, material failure, ground truth, required/critical checks, workload and success thresholds before running. Split challenge development from unseen evaluation; use an adjudication process independent of the producing models. Record compute cost, wall time and reviewer time with uncertainty and missingness.

Primary outcomes are validated discovery throughput, false-claim rate, independent reproduction, failure detection, audit reconstruction time, validation coverage, reviewer effort and robustness under a specified unseen attack distribution. The current descriptive evaluator covers the directly supplied throughput/error/reproduction/detection/audit/effort observations. Validation coverage, critical-claim completeness and unseen stability require additional domain instrumentation and adjudication, not invented default values.

The draft's illustrative targets (5× throughput; 95% reproduction and validation; all designated critical checks; 50% less audit time; zero violations of encoded invariants) are proposed acceptance criteria. None is an experimentally established result of this implementation. Passing controller tests does not establish zero violations in all possible deployments. Do not conflate a nondecreasing diagnostic potential with a formal convergence proof.

## Theory qualifications

The finite strict-descent argument and limitations of compact-state descent and metric-margin robustness are documented in the specification. A stable false belief is possible. Confidence, evidence density, software consistency, policy acceptance, empirical validity and permission to act remain different concepts.
